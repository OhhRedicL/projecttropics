Getting Started

How to Connect

  1. Install a legitimate PC copy of Grand Theft Auto V.
  2. Download and install the free FiveM client from fivem.net.
  3. Join our Discord and complete any required verification.
  4. Open FiveM, press F8, paste the connect command and press Enter.
Launch in FiveM
Server Connect

F8 Console Command

connect cfx.re/join/xxxxxx

Having Trouble?

Clear your FiveM cache, restart the client and try again. Still stuck? Open a support ticket in Discord.

FAQ

Frequently Asked

Q1Is Project Tropics whitelisted?

Department roles require an approved application. Check the Discord for the current server access requirements.

Q2Do I need to own GTA V?

Yes. You need a legitimate copy of Grand Theft Auto V for PC and the free FiveM client installed.

Q3Can I be in more than one department?

You may hold one primary department. Civilian Operations is open to everyone as a secondary role.

Q4How do I report a player?

Clip the incident and open a report ticket in our Discord. Never argue with players or staff in-game.

Q5I'm new to roleplay. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Our community and field training programs are built to help new roleplayers learn and grow.
