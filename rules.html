Community Rules

Our rules keep the city fair, fun and immersive for everyone. All members are expected to know and follow them.

Zero Tolerance

01General Community Rules

1.1Respect Everyone

Treat every member, staff member and guest with respect. Harassment, bullying, hate speech, discrimination or targeting of any kind is not tolerated in-game or on Discord.

1.2Staff Decisions Are Final

Staff decisions must be respected in the moment. If you disagree, open a ticket in Discord after the situation. Do not argue in-game or in public channels.

1.3Follow Platform Terms

All members must follow the Discord Terms of Service and the Cfx.re / FiveM Terms of Service. Cheating, exploiting or using mod menus results in a permanent ban.

1.4No Advertising

Advertising other servers or communities in-game, in DMs or on Discord is not allowed.

1.5Language

English must be used in voice and text so everyone can participate in the roleplay.

1.6Microphone Required

A working, clear microphone is required. Excessive background noise, music or voice changers are not allowed.

02Roleplay Standards

2.1Stay in Character

Remain in character at all times while in the city. Out-of-character (OOC) talk should be kept to a minimum and only used when necessary.

2.2Value of Life / Fear RP

Your character must value their life. If you are outnumbered or have a weapon pointed at you, react realistically and comply where a real person would.

2.3New Life Rule (NLR)

If your character is downed and respawns, you forget the events leading up to your death and may not return to that scene for at least 15 minutes.

2.4No Metagaming

Using information gained outside of roleplay (Discord, streams, OOC chat) to benefit your character in-game is strictly prohibited.

2.5No Powergaming

Do not force actions on other players without giving them a chance to respond, or perform actions that are unrealistic or impossible in real life.

2.6Realistic Driving

Drive realistically for the vehicle and situation. Off-roading in supercars, jumping cliffs or ramming without RP reason is not allowed.

2.7No Combat Logging

Disconnecting or leaving the server to avoid a roleplay situation, arrest or death is not allowed.

03Combat & Criminal Activity

3.1No RDM

Random Deathmatch, killing or attacking another player without a valid roleplay reason and proper initiation, is prohibited.

3.2No VDM

Vehicle Deathmatch, using a vehicle as a weapon to injure or kill players without valid roleplay reason, is prohibited.

3.3Proper Initiation

Before any hostile action, you must clearly initiate through voice or text roleplay and give the other party a reasonable chance to respond.

3.4Safe Zones

No criminal activity is allowed in hospitals, police stations, DOT yards or other posted safe zones.

3.5Cop Baiting

Deliberately provoking law enforcement for attention or chases without a roleplay purpose is not allowed.

3.6Criminal Limits

Major crimes (bank robberies, jewelry stores, etc.) require a minimum number of on-duty law enforcement and must follow posted crime cooldowns.

04Law Enforcement Conduct

4.1Follow Your SOP

All LAPD, LASD and CHP members must follow their department Standard Operating Procedures while on duty.

4.2Professional Conduct

Officers represent their department. Treat civilians, suspects and allied agencies professionally at all times.

4.3Use of Force

Use force that is reasonable for the situation. Lethal force should only be used when there is an immediate threat to life.

4.4No Unapproved Corruption

Corrupt officer roleplay is not allowed without written approval from department leadership.

4.5Off-Duty Equipment

Department vehicles, weapons and uniforms may not be used while off duty.

05Discord Rules

5.1Keep It Clean

No NSFW, gore or offensive content anywhere on the Discord server.

5.2No Spam

Do not spam messages, emojis, mentions or links.

5.3Use Tickets

Reports, appeals and support requests must be submitted through the ticket system, not staff DMs.

5.4Correct Channels

Keep conversations in the correct channels and keep in-character discussion out of general chat.
