01General Community Rules

1.1Purpose

These Global Standard Operating Procedures set the community-wide rules for Project Tropics Roleplay (PTRP). They apply to every member who plays on the FiveM server, uses PTRP Discord spaces, or represents the community in any way.

PTRP is an emergency-services-focused community built around realistic, well-structured roleplay. Civilian roleplay is open to everyone. Whitelisted departments are open only to members who have been approved and trained.

1.2Agreement to the Rules

By taking part in PTRP, you agree to follow these rules, reasonable staff direction, any department policy that applies to you, and the spirit in which the rules are written. Hunting for loopholes or technicalities is not acceptable.

Not having read a published rule does not excuse breaking it.

1.3Community Structure

PTRP keeps community-wide rules separate from department SOPs. These Global SOPs apply to everyone. Each department may add its own policies covering training, pursuits, radio procedure, uniforms, equipment, and chain of command, as long as nothing in them conflicts with these Global SOPs.

1.4Respect & Behavior

Treat other members with basic respect. Harassment, targeted bullying, hate speech, discrimination, threats, doxxing, repeated personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive toxicity are all prohibited.

Keep disagreements about the issue itself. Do not let in-character conflict become a real personal feud.

1.5Staff Interactions

When staff are handling a situation, follow their instructions and let them work. Do not interfere with an investigation, dodge moderation, or argue publicly about an active punishment.

If you disagree with a staff decision, use the ticket or appeal process.

1.6Follow Platform Terms

All members must follow the Discord Terms of Service and the Cfx.re / FiveM Terms of Service.

1.7Exploits, Cheats & Abuse

Cheats, mod menus, unauthorized injectors, duplication exploits, script abuse, animation abuse, and knowingly using a bug for advantage are prohibited.

If you find a serious exploit, report it to staff. Do not demonstrate it or share it with others.

1.8Advertising & Solicitation

Advertising other FiveM communities, Discord servers, paid services, or competing communities without authorization is prohibited. Members may not stream or screen share another FiveM or roleplay server in PTRP voice channels, tickets, or other community spaces.

Casual conversation about other communities is not automatically advertising. Recruiting PTRP members away, repeatedly promoting another community, or showing another server by screen share is.

1.9Language

English must be used in voice and text so everyone can participate in the roleplay.

1.10Microphone Required

A working, clear microphone is required. Voice changers are not allowed.

1.11Fictional Use

PTRP is a fictional roleplay community operating within Grand Theft Auto V and FiveM. These procedures are for game and community use only and are not real-world legal, law enforcement, medical, or emergency-services guidance.

02Roleplay Standards

2.1Stay in Character

Remain in character at all times while in the city. Out-of-character (OOC) talk should be kept to a minimum and only used when necessary.

2.2FailRP

FailRP is behavior that is clearly unrealistic, breaks immersion, or does not fit the circumstances of the scene. Examples include ignoring serious injuries, using vehicles in impossible ways, recovering instantly from major trauma, or refusing to engage with obvious consequences.

2.3Powergaming

Do not force an outcome onto another player without giving them a fair chance to respond. Your roleplay should leave room for others to react unless game mechanics clearly decide the result.

2.4Metagaming

Do not use out-of-character information to gain an in-character advantage. This includes stream sniping, acting on information from unrelated Discord conversations, and receiving live scene details from someone your character is not legitimately talking to.

2.5FearRP / Value of Life

Characters must value their lives. Being outnumbered or held at gunpoint should change how your character behaves. FearRP does not mean automatic surrender every time, but acting as if serious danger does not exist is FailRP.

2.6New Life Rule (NLR)

After your character dies and respawns, you may not return to the same active scene, seek revenge, or act on detailed knowledge of what led to your death. Staff may set or adjust an NLR period when a scene calls for it.

2.7Combat Logging

Leaving the server to avoid arrest, a roleplay consequence, death, staff action, or an active scene is prohibited. If you disconnect unexpectedly, make a reasonable effort to return or tell the people involved or staff.

2.8Vehicle Realism

Drive within reasonable real-world limits for the vehicle and situation. Stunt driving, unrealistic jumps, taking ordinary vehicles over extreme terrain, and treating serious crashes as meaningless are FailRP.

2.9Injury Roleplay

Serious crashes, shootings, falls, assaults, and other major injuries must have reasonable roleplay consequences that match their severity and location. You do not need to simulate medical detail, but you must not ignore the injury.

2.10Scene Conduct

Do not crowd into, hijack, or insert yourself into active scenes without a legitimate roleplay reason. Do not go looking for scenes only to get clips, gunfights, pursuits, or attention.

2.11Restricted Roleplay

Sexual roleplay, explicit sexual content, torture-focused roleplay, suicide or self-harm roleplay, and terrorism or extremism roleplay are prohibited unless PTRP Ownership has published a narrower, approved policy for a specific scenario. Do not introduce restricted themes as a surprise in ordinary scenes.

03Combat & Criminal Activity

3.1Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Attacking, injuring, or killing another player without a legitimate roleplay reason or real roleplay interaction beforehand is prohibited. Every use of force needs enough context that the other player can understand why it is happening.

3.2Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Using a vehicle as a weapon without a legitimate, realistic reason is prohibited. Deliberately running people over, repeatedly ramming vehicles, or using a vehicle to kill with no valid scene reason may be treated as VDM.

3.3Proper Initiation

Before any hostile action, you must clearly initiate through voice or text roleplay and give the other party a reasonable chance to respond.

3.4Firearm & Equipment Realism

Large weapons and equipment must be obtained and carried realistically. Rifles, shotguns, shields, breaching tools, and similar items should not appear out of nowhere. Use weapons in a way that fits the scene, and do not turn a minor disagreement into an instant shootout without believable escalation.

3.5Criminal Limits

Major crimes (bank robberies, jewelry stores, etc.) require a minimum number of on-duty law enforcement.

3.6Priority Scenes

Priority scenes are major incidents that need focused emergency-service attention, such as shootings, hostage situations, major robberies, and large manhunts. Members must follow the active priority system and any cooldown the server displays.

3.7Priority Abuse

Do not chain major violent scenes back to back, interrupt another active priority, ignore cooldowns, or escalate a minor situation just to create a priority.

04Civilian Operations

4.1Open Access

Civilian roleplay is open to everyone. You do not need to join a department to take part, subject to PTRP age and access requirements and community rules.

Open access is not unrestricted roleplay. Civilians are held to the same standards of conduct, realism, priority, and scene quality as every other member.

4.2Criminal Roleplay

Criminal roleplay should be story-driven and believable. Avoid nonstop violent crime, repeated cop-baiting, robbery spam, forcing pursuits, and treating every police interaction as a reason to run or shoot.

4.3Civilian Vehicles & Characters

Civilian vehicles and characters must fit PTRP's intended level of realism. Staff may stop or remove meme vehicles, immersion-breaking liveries, troll characters, and unrealistic vehicle setups that disrupt roleplay.

4.4Impersonating Emergency Services

Civilians may not present themselves as an on-duty PTRP law enforcement officer, firefighter, EMS member, or other whitelisted public-safety member unless they are authorized for that role. Civilian characters may portray lawful private-sector or fictional roles as long as they do not falsely claim whitelisted department authority.

05Whitelisted Departments

5.1Whitelist Requirement

All PTRP public-safety departments are whitelisted. You must be accepted, trained, and authorized by the department before going on duty as a member of it.

Owning a uniform, vehicle, callsign, or EUP item does not give you department membership or authority.

5.2Department Applications

New applicants must use the department's official application and onboarding process, unless they qualify for an approved transfer under Transfers, Promotions & Rank.

5.3Follow Your SOP

All LAPD, LASD and CHP members must follow their department Standard Operating Procedures while on duty.

5.4Professional Conduct

Department members must act professionally while on duty, follow proper roleplay procedure, and avoid abusing department tools, weapons, vehicles, permissions, or authority.

5.5Use of Force

Force must be reasonably tied to the threat presented in the scene. Excessive, punitive, or clearly unrealistic force is prohibited. Department use-of-force policies may be stricter than this rule.

5.6Pursuits

Pursuits must balance realism, public safety, and the seriousness of the offense. Supervisors may end unsafe or unrealistic pursuits. Each department's SOP may set unit limits, intervention techniques, and pursuit tactics.

5.7Corruption & Abuse

Unauthorized corruption, evidence planting, knowingly false arrests, personal targeting, and misuse of police powers are prohibited. Any corruption storyline must have explicit approval from the appropriate PTRP authority before it begins.

5.8Off-Duty Equipment

Department vehicles, weapons and uniforms may not be used while off duty.

5.9Department Recruiting & Poaching

Zero-tolerance policy. Departments and their members may not recruit behind the scenes, pressure, or poach members from another PTRP department. Anyone who tries to pull members out of another department through unofficial recruiting or similar conduct may be permanently removed from PTRP departments.

5.10Fire, Rescue & EMS Access

Only authorized members may represent a whitelisted fire, rescue, or EMS department while on duty. When that department is not staffed, civilians and law enforcement should handle medical outcomes reasonably within available server mechanics.

5.11Fire, Rescue & EMS Scene Conduct

Fire, rescue, and EMS personnel must coordinate with law enforcement when a scene is unsafe, maintain reasonable scene safety, and deliver professional medical and fire roleplay.

06Transfers, Promotions & Rank

6.1Department Transfers

A member in good standing may request a transfer from one PTRP department to another. An approved transfer takes the place of a brand-new application unless the receiving department or PTRP Management requires one for a specific reason.

Transfers are not automatic. The receiving department may review disciplinary history, activity, training status, qualifications, and current staffing before accepting.

6.2Rank Reset on Transfer

All transfers start at the bottom of the receiving department's rank structure. A previous PTRP rank, command role, certification, staff position, friendship, donation status, or rank from another community does not entitle anyone to an equal or higher rank in the new department.

For example, a Lieutenant who transfers does not arrive as a Lieutenant. They start at the entry rank and earn advancement through the new department.

6.3Training & Certifications After Transfer

A receiving department may recognize prior experience to speed up training, but it is not required to honor certifications, supervisor status, specialty assignments, or command qualifications. Department leadership decides what must be retrained or recertified.

6.4Promotions & Free Ranking

Ranks must be earned through the receiving department's established process. Free ranking, favoritism, and handing out command positions because of a member's prior rank, staff role, friendship, relationship, donation, or outside-community status is prohibited.

PTRP Ownership may make organizational appointments when needed to establish or rebuild a department. These are administrative exceptions, not transfer privileges.

6.5Department SOP Authority

Department leadership controls internal training, promotions, certifications, subdivisions, scheduling, and operational policy within the limits of PTRP rules. No department policy may override these Global SOPs.

07Safe Zones, Events & AOP

7.1Safe Zones

Staff may designate spawn areas, training locations, official event areas, and other places as temporary or permanent safe zones. Unless an event says otherwise, hostile actions, theft, trolling, and violence are prohibited inside an active safe zone.

7.2Official Events

Follow all event-specific instructions. Deliberately disrupting memorials, ceremonies, trainings, community events, or staff-run scenes may lead to heavier punishment depending on severity and intent.

7.3Area of Patrol (AOP)

When an AOP is active, keep your main roleplay inside the announced area unless a scene naturally carries you outside it or staff authorize otherwise. Only authorized staff may make an official AOP change.

08Discord, Voice & Communications

8.1Keep It Clean

No NSFW, gore or offensive content anywhere on the Discord server.

8.2Voice Conduct

Keep your voice communication clear and reasonably controlled. Mic spam, loud noise, soundboard abuse, deliberate voice disruption, and screaming meant to annoy others are prohibited.

8.3In-Game and Discord Separation

Do not use Discord voice to get around in-game radio or proximity systems during active roleplay. If you are in a Discord voice channel while playing, do not relay in-game conversations through it in a way that creates metagaming.

8.4Discord Channels

Use each channel for its intended purpose. Do not spam mentions, abuse the ticket system, flood channels, impersonate staff, or stir up community drama.

8.5Use Tickets

Never DM staff with reports, appeals or support requests. Report rule breaks in game with /report, or open a ticket in Discord if you are no longer in game. Appeals and support requests go through the Discord ticket system.

8.6Department Communications

Department radios and official department channels should stay professional enough to support active operations. Department leadership may set additional radio and communication standards.

09Staff Authority, Enforcement & Appeals

9.1Staff Authority

Staff may step in whenever conduct harms roleplay, community safety, server stability, or fair play, even if the exact behavior is not listed here. Staff will apply the closest applicable rule and consider context.

9.2Administrative Actions

Possible actions include verbal direction, warnings, kicks, temporary bans, permanent bans, department discipline referrals, whitelist removal, and other reasonable restrictions.

Punishment depends on severity, intent, prior history, impact, cooperation, and whether the conduct was repeated. PTRP does not promise a fixed punishment ladder for every violation.

9.3Serious Violations

Cheating, malicious exploitation, doxxing, credible threats, severe harassment, ban evasion, and deliberate attacks on PTRP infrastructure may result in immediate permanent removal.

9.4Staff Discretion & Consistency

Decisions made by staff during an active situation must be followed and may be reviewed afterward through the appeal process. Staff are also bound by PTRP rules and may be reviewed for misuse of authority.

9.5Appeal Process

Members may appeal eligible punishments through the official PTRP support or appeal system. An appeal should explain what happened, identify the action being appealed, and include relevant evidence where available.

9.6Appeal Conduct

Appeals must be truthful and respectful. Fabricated evidence, harassment, spam appeals, and attempts to pressure staff may lead to denial or further administrative action.

9.7Rule Interpretation

These rules exist to protect realistic, fair, and enjoyable roleplay, not to create loopholes. Staff may interpret unclear situations according to the purpose of this document.

9.8Updates

PTRP may revise these Global SOPs as the community grows. The current published version replaces all earlier PTRP global rule documents.
