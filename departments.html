Los Angeles Communications

LA Communications is home to our dispatchers, the voice behind every call in the city. Dispatchers answer 911 calls, send the right units, track every officer on the map and keep pursuits and major scenes under control. If you like being at the center of the action and keeping everyone organized, take a seat at the console.

No FiveM needed

You don't need FiveM or to be in game. Dispatchers use our CAD and radio dispatch panel from outside the city. All you need is Discord and a working mic.

Positions

911 Call TakerPolice DispatchHighway DispatchTow & DOT DispatchCommunications Supervisor

What You Will Do

  • Answer 911 calls and create incidents in our CAD, from anywhere
  • Dispatch LAPD, LASD, CHP and DOT units to calls
  • Run plates and warrants, track unit status and coordinate pursuits

Department of Transportation

The DOT keeps Los Angeles moving. Crews respond to collisions, tow disabled and impounded vehicles, set up traffic control and clean up after major scenes. DOT works hand in hand with every law enforcement agency in the city.

Divisions

Tow & RecoveryRoadside AssistanceTraffic ControlRoad MaintenanceImpound

What You Will Do

  • Respond to collisions and tow requests from law enforcement
  • Set up road closures, detours and traffic control
  • Clear scenes and keep the freeways flowing
