Los Angeles Police Department

The LAPD is the backbone of public safety in the City of Los Angeles. Officers handle everything from traffic stops and domestic calls to armed robberies and major investigations. If you want fast-paced city policing with room to specialize, this is your home.

Divisions

PatrolMetro / SWATDetective BureauTraffic DivisionK-9 UnitAir Support

What You Will Do

  • Patrol the city and respond to 911 calls for service
  • Conduct traffic stops, arrests and investigations
  • Train through an FTO program and earn certifications

Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department

LASD deputies patrol the vast unincorporated areas of LA County, from desert towns to mountain roads and coastal communities. Deputies enjoy a wide variety of calls, close-knit stations and some of the most scenic patrol areas in the city.

Divisions

PatrolSpecial Enforcement BureauDetectivesSearch & RescueMarine UnitAero Bureau

What You Will Do

  • Patrol county areas and back up allied agencies
  • Handle rural, mountain and coastal calls for service
  • Advance into specialty units through training and merit

California Highway Patrol

The CHP keeps California's highways and freeways safe. Troopers specialize in traffic enforcement, DUI interdiction, pursuit operations and collision investigation. If you love vehicles and high-speed action, the open road is calling.

Divisions

Road PatrolMotor UnitCommercial EnforcementMAITAir OperationsSpecial Response Team

What You Will Do

  • Enforce traffic laws on freeways and state routes
  • Lead pursuits and coordinate PIT and spike-strip tactics
  • Investigate major collisions and assist motorists

Civilian Operations

Civilians are the heartbeat of Los Angeles. Without them, there is no city. Build a character with a real backstory, open a business, start a crew, work a legal career or take your chances on the wrong side of the law. The only limit is your creativity.

Paths

Business OwnersLegal CareersStreet Crews & GangsOrganized CrimeCar CultureEveryday Citizens

What You Will Do

  • Create unique characters with their own goals and stories
  • Drive the city's economy, events and storylines
  • Start approved businesses and organizations

Department of Transportation

The DOT keeps Los Angeles moving. Crews respond to collisions, tow disabled and impounded vehicles, set up traffic control and clean up after major scenes. DOT works hand in hand with every law enforcement agency in the city.

Divisions

Tow & RecoveryRoadside AssistanceTraffic ControlRoad MaintenanceImpound

What You Will Do

  • Respond to collisions and tow requests from law enforcement
  • Set up road closures, detours and traffic control
  • Clear scenes and keep the freeways flowing
