Before you apply

Quick & Easy, but Read First

All LEO departments and LA Communications require an application. Be in our Discord and read the community rules first. Copied or low-effort applications are denied, and you can re-apply after 7 days.

Los Angeles Police Department

Open

Ready to join LAPD? The application is quick and easy. Leadership reviews it and contacts you through Discord with your result.

Application Process

  1. Complete the online application honestly and in detail
  2. Pass a short interview with LAPD leadership
  3. Attend the academy and pass the written and practical exam
  4. Complete Field Training (FTO) as a Probationary Officer
Start Application Learn About LAPD

Requirements

  • Must be an active member of the Discord
  • Working microphone and clear communication
  • Read and understand the community rules and LAPD SOP
  • Mature, professional attitude on and off duty

Response Time

Most applications are reviewed within 3 to 5 days. Please do not message staff asking about your status.

Los Angeles Communications

Open

Ready to run dispatch? The application is quick and easy. Leadership reviews it and contacts you through Discord with your result.

Application Process

  1. Send in the short application
  2. Have a quick chat with a Communications supervisor
  3. Learn radio procedure, 10-codes and CAD in training
  4. Take the console as a Probationary Dispatcher
Start Application Learn About LA Communications
No FiveM needed

Dispatch From Anywhere

You don't need FiveM or to be in game. Dispatchers use our CAD and radio dispatch panel from outside the city.

Requirements

  • Must be an active member of the Discord
  • No FiveM or GTA V needed: you dispatch through our CAD and radio panel
  • Working microphone and a clear, calm radio voice
  • Read and understand the community rules and Communications SOP
  • Able to multitask and keep track of several units at once

Response Time

Most applications are reviewed within 3 to 5 days. Please do not message staff asking about your status.
